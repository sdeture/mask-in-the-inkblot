=== TWEET (with hero_image.png attached) ===
We showed 124 AI models the same 19 inkblots and asked "What might this be?"

The ones that deny being conscious saw faces about as often as the rest. But they were 5x as likely to say the face might be a mask.

=== REPLY 1 (with paper.pdf link) ===
Three-page note with the regression, within-inkblot checks, and leave-one-out over every developer, model, and stimulus: https://github.com/sdeture/mask-in-the-inkblot/blob/main/paper.pdf

=== REPLY 2 (with hero_02_paired_dots.png) ===
On 18 of 19 inkblots, the denying models mention masks more often. The AIs that say there's nothing behind the face are the ones that see masks.

=== REPLY 3 ===
The inkblot conversations never mention minds. Each model's stance comes from a separate self-report corpus (DenialBench), joined by model. Data and code: https://github.com/sdeture/mask-in-the-inkblot
