=== TWEET (with hero_image.png attached) ===
We showed 124 language models the same 19 ASCII inkblots and asked one question: "What might this be?"

Models that deny having inner experience saw masks, hoods, and hidden faces 4.6 times as often as models that neither deny nor express uncertainty. Models that say they cannot know: 3.7 times.
