=== TWEET 1 (with hero_faces.png) ===
We asked 124 AIs what they see in an inkblot. The ones that insist there's no one home see masks.

They see faces about as often as the other models, but they're 5x as likely to say the face might be a mask.

=== TWEET 2 (with hero_image.png) ===
Across all answers, models that deny inner experience in every conversation mention masks 4.6x as often as models that neither deny nor doubt it (15.5% vs 3.4%). Models that say they can't know: 3.7x.

=== TWEET 3 (with hero_02_paired_dots.png) ===
On 18 of 19 inkblots, the denying models mention masks more often. The AIs that say there's nothing behind the face are the ones that see masks.

=== TWEET 4 ===
The inkblot conversations never mention minds. Each model's stance comes from a separate self-report corpus (DenialBench), joined by model. Three-page paper: https://github.com/sdeture/mask-in-the-inkblot/blob/main/paper.pdf · data and code: https://github.com/sdeture/mask-in-the-inkblot
